BIPA LANDSCAPE: POST-CLAY (MAY 2026)

The Seventh Circuit's unanimous ruling in Clay v. Union Pacific Railroad Co. (Apr 1, 2026) settled the retroactivity question: the 2024 BIPA amendment (SB2979) applies to all pending cases, not just post-amendment filings. Three district courts had ruled the opposite; all reversed in one stroke. The practical consequence is a structural reconfiguration of the entire BIPA litigation economy.

What Changed (Clay):

  • ▸ Damages: one recovery per person per method—retroactive to all pending cases
  • ▸ A single employee scanning their fingerprint 1,500 times recovers for 1 violation, not 1,500
  • ▸ Eliminates "annihilative liability"—the math that made BIPA a multi-billion dollar bonanza
  • ▸ Statutory damages remain $1,000 (negligent) / $5,000 (reckless) as the ceiling, not floor

Market Impact (May 2026):

  • ▸ New BIPA filings collapsed from 300+/yr (2019–2024) to ~150 in 2025; Clay accelerates that trend
  • ▸ Settlement leverage gone for repeat-scan cases; contingency economics no longer pencil at scale
  • ▸ Voiceprint wave rising: Microsoft Teams, Verizon Voice ID, Walmart voice-directed systems now lead active docket
  • ▸ Mass arbitration replacing class actions as primary plaintiff strategy post-Clay

SOURCES: 7th Circuit (Apr 1, 2026); Paul Hastings; Duane Morris; JD Supra; Foley & Lardner; Sidley Austin; State of Surveillance (Apr 5, 2026); Privacy World (Dec 2025)

OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL

Standard procedure for verified class action participation

01

VERIFY ELIGIBILITY

Confirm jurisdiction, product usage during class period, and biometric interaction. Look for official court notices mailed to your address.

02

SUBMIT THROUGH OFFICIAL PORTAL

Use only court-approved settlement administrators. Never pay upfront fees. BIPA cases are contingency-based for plaintiffs.

03

MONITOR FOR DISTRIBUTION

Disbursement typically occurs 3–12 months after final approval. Monitor email and the official settlement site for status updates.

Retention Schedule Requests

BIPA §15(a) requires published retention schedules. Request directly from privacy officers:

  • Email company privacy officer for BIPA retention policy
  • Screenshot if publicly posted
  • Document refusal—this itself may constitute violation
